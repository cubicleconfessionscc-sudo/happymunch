How it works

Three steps from craving to lunch — no queue required.

1

Browse & order

Log in with your employee ID, pick a cafeteria vendor and add items to your cart. Pay online (test mode) in seconds.

2

Pick your slot

Choose a pickup time that suits you. The kitchen sees your order instantly and starts preparing it ahead of time.

3

Collect & munch

Show your QR code at the counter at your slot time. The vendor scans or enters your order number — done.
