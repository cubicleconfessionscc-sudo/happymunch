How it works

Three steps from craving to lunch — no queue required.

1

Browse & order

Log in with your employee ID, pick a cafeteria vendor and add items to your cart. It takes seconds.

2

Pay with UPI

Tap Pay to open GPay, PhonePe or Paytm with the vendor's number and amount pre-filled. Online payment only — no cash.

3

Pick up & munch

Choose a pickup slot, show your QR at the counter at your time. The food is ready and waiting.
